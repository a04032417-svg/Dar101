# Therapy private practice suite — eight parts (all share th_core.py: owner-clinician, 2 employed clinicians, 2 contract clinicians, 1 supervised associate, 1 practice coordinator, 5 outside contacts, 5 rooms plus online, ~450 sessions a month, ~2 years of history)

The demo business is "Cedar Bridge Therapy Group" (fictional): a small group practice of talking therapists and counsellors run by an owner-clinician (Amira Lindqvist, TH-01). Two clinicians are employed on salary (Daniel Okoye, Sofia Marchetti), two contract clinicians are paid a share of each session fee (Kenji Watanabe 60%, Priya Raman 65%, who runs speech and behaviour therapy and joined about 16 months ago), one supervised associate is paid per session (Lucas Moreau, joined about 10 months ago) and a practice coordinator runs the front desk, scheduling and billing (Grace Mensah, hourly). Five outside contacts: accountant, external clinical supervisor, IT support, landlord and cleaner. Five therapy rooms (Garden, Willow, Family, Play and Quiet Room) plus online sessions; individual, couples, family and child sessions plus speech and behaviour therapy; self-pay, sliding-scale, package and third-party payer clients. Each part is a standalone workbook that carries its own copies of the reference tables it needs. Charts group by month or by a list column whose values the demo data uses fully (clinician, service, room, payer, status, reason, source, kind, category, program); never by a calc column, an id, a client name, a quarter or a year list, and never with hundreds of rows as an axis. A table with fewer than 12 rows may have only 3 columns: design for that (Services, Payers, Rooms, Tenants, Programs). Privacy: the workbook holds status only — a note's done date and on-time flag, a review due date, an outcome score as a plain number — never note content, a diagnosis, a clinical claim or treatment advice; privacy, consent and record-keeping items are generic checklists that say "check your own local rules". No payer, insurer, platform, software, bank or company names and no law, agency, licence or standard names anywhere.
