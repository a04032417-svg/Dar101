# STUDY — small sublimation print shop / custom drinkware and gifts business (market and workflow)

Method: 27 web calls on 2026-11-15 (slightly over the 25 cap: 11 searches, 16 fetches; 1 fetch blocked by the allowlist, 1 redirected). Built on Research 19 in research6_20261115.md (which already found the Etsy cost-calculator listing with 4 reviews; not repeated except in the table). Many search results returned only titles, so figures come from fetched pages. Brand names appear in this study only as research sources; none may reach the product. Anything not found is marked NOT verified.

## 1. Real jobs and pain points

**Daily jobs (owner, helper, packer, designer):** answer enquiries and quote requests; build and send proofs, collect approval (no print before approval); take deposits; check the day's queue against press capacity; pull blanks for each job and check count against the order; print transfers, check colour and alignment; press (tumbler, mug, flat, apparel), cool, inspect; log misprints and the reason; wrap, label and pack; update the stock count of blanks used; post tracking and tell the customer; take payments and balances at pick-up.
**Weekly jobs:** plan next week's queue by due date and press hours; reorder check on blanks, paper, ink, tape, shrink wrap, boxes against reorder points and supplier lead times; chase unapproved proofs and unpaid balances; quote follow-ups; test-press and clean the presses and printer (nozzle check, head clean, waste-ink tank check); batch the week's online orders; review misprint log; look at cash.
**Monthly jobs:** profit per item and per order check (blank + ink + paper + tape + packaging + press time + design time + fees + shipping); ink and paper use per print against last month; supplier stock take and dead-stock list; repeat-customer and bulk-order review; wholesale price list review for teams, schools, churches and businesses; marketplace and payment fees total; ad and market spend; pay run for helper; owner pay and tax set-aside transfer; books for the accountant.
**Yearly and seasonal jobs:** seasonal build (holiday gifts, teacher gifts, back to school, sports seasons, school-year-end, wedding season); pre-season blank buy and deposit with suppliers; market and craft-fair calendar (booth fee, travel, sales, stock taken and returned); price review at least twice a year (cost sheets) and quarterly price check; equipment replacement plan (press, printer, heads, mug press elements); insurance and permit renewals (generic: "your local rules"); year-end pack for the accountant; design library clean-up.
**Order-level jobs:** quote for custom and bulk orders; minimum order quantities and set-up or design fees; rush fee rule; deposits and balances; proof and approval log; production slot booking; reprint decision (who pays); delivery or pick-up; repeat-order re-quote from the saved design.

**Pain points reported (sources):**
- Underpricing is "perhaps the biggest mistake" decorators make; owners use volume mark-ups without knowing true costs; more than 60% of new sellers underprice in year one (one guide; weak, no method given). https://screenprintingmag.com/top-5-mistakes-made-by-sublimation-decorators/ ; https://www.printablepress.com/how-to-price-sublimation-products/
- Guessing prices and forgetting rush fees and minimums; update prices quarterly. https://jotoimagingsupplies.ca/blogs/blogs/mistakes-new-print-shops-make
- Wasted blanks and ink from bad files (72 dpi image, no bleed), wrong blanks and a cheap press that "ruins half the prints"; do not start custom work without a deposit. same page
- Hard to work out cost per print from ink: "Very difficult to work out how many mugs you'll get ... cost really is pennies"; one owner assumed 25p a mug for paper and ink, another estimated 70p; another reported "going through a set of carts a week which is nearly a grand a month" before changing ink. https://dyesubforum.co.uk/viewtopic.php?p=127382 (forum, owner's own words)
- Ink held in printer lines means first sets of cartridges "won't appear to last long"; low-ink warning "can last quite a while". same thread
- Misprints and reprints: ghosting (paper moved), faded colour, blurry edges, uneven transfer (platen corner variance over 10 F), wrong substrate; build 5-10% error allowance for blanks still being learned. https://www.printablepress.com/how-to-fix-sublimation-mistakes/
- First 50 tumblers cost an estimated 200-500 (CAD) in mistakes. https://secure.lamose.com/blogs/recipes/custom-tumbler-business-profit-margins-real-numbers (supplier blog; weak)
- A gross margin of 70% becomes 50-55% net after 15-25% of costs; an example month of 40 units ends at about 40% net and about 28 an hour. same page
- Craft fairs: sales depend on event size and conversion (2-20% guess); owners struggle to cover the booth fee. https://www.madeurban.com/blog/how-much-money-can-make-craft-show/ ; https://www.madeurban.com/blog/what-to-do-when-craft-fair-sales-dont-cover-booth-fees/ (title only read)
- Software that spreadsheets cannot match is said to be: automatic costs, actual purchase prices, overhead allocation (vendor claim): spreadsheets use static costs and get hard past 10-15 SKUs. https://craftybase.com/etsy-pricing-calculator-spreadsheet (marketing claim; use as the bar to beat)
- NOT verified: Reddit and Facebook-group threads (searches returned articles and a UK forum, not readable social posts); seasonal share of sales; repeat-customer and bulk-order share.

## 2. Leading print-shop / order-management and craft-business software (research only)
- Print-shop order software A: one flat plan at 299 a month, no trial listed (12 reviews, value 3.5/5). Features: catalogue and configurator, quotes and cost estimating, order tracking, approvals, payments, inventory, reports. Complaints: "huge price increases without clear notification", "complicated and expensive", limited customisation, no bulk-add of products, learning curve, glitches, feature removed without notice. https://www.capterra.com/p/149085/InkSoft/
- Print-shop software B: 109 a month (2 users), 244 (5 users), custom quote for 20 users plus 19 per extra user; 7-day trial. Features: job calendar, quotes and invoices, approvals, product catalogues, group-order stores, analytics. Complaints: user limits, no automatic job run times, limited permissions; one reviewer said it is built for screen-print, embroidery and signs, and a digital and wide-format shop found it a poor fit (1 review only). https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/printavo/
- Craft-business inventory and cost software C: 14-day trial; prices on a separate page NOT verified (pricing page blocked). It sells automatic cost of goods from actual purchases and says spreadsheets are static. https://craftybase.com/etsy-pricing-calculator-spreadsheet
- Other print-shop listings seen but not opened: web-to-print stores (InfoFlo, OnPrintShop, ImprintNext, PrintCommerce etc.) are aimed at larger commercial printers. https://www.softwareadvice.com/print-estimating/print-shop-management-profile/
Hidden costs: per-user pricing, price rises, add-ons, payment fees, built for screen print/embroidery not for blanks-and-press sublimation (no blanks-by-size-and-colour stock, no ink per print, no press-capacity day).
**What a spreadsheet must do better:** one flat 29 dollars once, no per-user or monthly fee; cost per item built from blank, ink, paper, tape, packaging, press time, design time, fees; blanks stock by type, size and colour with reorder alerts; press capacity per day with a queue; quote with deposit and bulk tiers in one page; misprint log that feeds true cost; market-day sheet; verdict tools ("Take this order? Raise price? Reorder now?"); owner keeps the data; works in Excel and Google Sheets with no macros.

## Gaps and improvements for the blueprint
(Most valuable first. Every item generic; no brand, law or licence names. A "tool" ends with a verdict.)

1. **True cost per item, full stack.** Cost sheet per product: blank, ink (ml times price per ml), paper (sheets), tape, shrink wrap, packaging, press time, design time, shipping materials, a waste allowance; output cost, price, gross and net margin, verdict "price OK / raise / drop".
2. **Cost per ink ml from real purchases.** Enter bottle price, ml, and prints done to give ink per print by item type; compare to the assumed ml; flag drift.
3. **Quote for a custom or bulk order.** Item, quantity, tier price, design fee, set-up fee, rush fee, deposit; shows margin per piece and total; verdict "take / counter / decline" against a margin floor.
4. **Minimum order and break-even quantity.** For each product, quantity where design and set-up time are covered; sets the minimum order suggestion.
5. **Rush-fee calculator.** Rush percentage needed to pay for displaced jobs and overtime; verdict if rush is worth taking.
6. **Bulk tier builder.** Volume tiers (for example 1-10, 11-25, 26+) with margin at each tier and a warning below the net margin floor (default 40%).
7. **Wholesale price list for teams, schools, churches and businesses.** Printable list built from cost plus wholesale margin; compare retail, wholesale and bulk side by side per item.
8. **Order intake sheet with proof and approval status.** One row per order: status (quote, proof sent, approved, in production, packed, delivered), due date, with a rule "no print before approved".
9. **Deposit and balance tracker.** Deposit percentage rule, balance due, aged balances (0-7, 8-30, 30+ days), chase list; verdict "hold production until deposit paid".
10. **Production queue by due date.** Rank jobs by due date and press minutes; shows what is late and what can start today.
11. **Press capacity per day.** Prints per hour per press and hours per day, with setup and cool-down; shows booked share of capacity and a red flag over the cap; "Can I take this rush order by Friday?" verdict.
12. **Capacity forecast for the season.** Weeks to peak, expected orders, press hours needed vs available; verdict "hire help / extend hours / close order book".
13. **Misprint and reprint log.** Reason list (ghosting, faded, blur, wrong file, blank defect, operator), cost of each, who pays; rate per month with a target and a trend.
14. **Misprint cost loaded into price.** Auto-add the owner's own rolling misprint rate to each item's cost; compare with the 5-10% learning range.
15. **Blanks stock by type, size and colour.** Count in, used (from orders), on hand, value; one view per supplier.
16. **Reorder points and lead times.** Reorder point = daily use times lead time plus safety; list "order now", with case-size rounding.
17. **Pre-season buy planner.** Forecast blanks from last year's units and growth; cash needed; verdict if buying a bigger case lowers cost enough versus cash tied up.
18. **Dead-stock and slow-mover list.** Blanks with no use for 90 days, value tied up, suggested markdown or bundle.
19. **Supplier price comparison.** Price per blank by quantity tier, shipping, lead time, landed cost; ranked list; breakeven quantity for a bigger buy.
20. **Ink, paper and consumables use per month.** Ink ml and paper sheets used against prints made; cost per print trend; alert when use per print rises (head clogs, waste tank).
21. **Equipment upkeep and replacement planner.** Press, printer, heads, mug elements: purchase price, life in prints, cost per print, replace date; verdict "repair or replace".
22. **Return on a new press or printer.** Extra capacity times margin per print against price; payback in months; scenario with 10 / 20 / 30% fewer orders.
23. **Margin check per order.** After every order: price, actual cost incl. misprints, fees, shipping; flag orders under the margin floor and list the worst five each month.
24. **Margin check per item type.** Ranked list: tumblers, mugs, shirts, signs, coasters, puzzles, bottles by margin per press minute (not only per item); shows which to push.
25. **Margin per hour of work.** Net profit divided by hours (press, design, packing); verdict against the owner's wanted hourly rate (one example month reached about 28 an hour; do not hard-code).
26. **Price test.** Add 10% to chosen items for 30 days; compare units and profit before and after from the order log.
27. **Online marketplace and payment fee calculator (generic).** Owner enters fee rates; shows fee share of price, net per item, and the lowest price that still hits the margin target, by sales channel (market, own website, marketplace, direct order).
28. **Shipping cost per order.** Box size, weight, postage band, packaging cost; shows loss when "free shipping" is offered; verdict on a free-shipping threshold.
29. **Market and craft-fair day planner.** Fee, travel, booth costs, stock taken, expected buyers times conversion times average sale; verdict "go / skip" and break-even sales.
30. **Market-day result log.** Stock taken, sold, returned, takings, fees, margin; compare event against own history and rank the events by profit per hour.
31. **Repeat-customer register.** Customer, orders, spend, last order date, saved design; list of customers due a re-order message (teams each season, teachers each year).
32. **Reorder offer for repeat bulk customers.** Quote from the saved previous order with updated costs; shows margin change if costs have moved.
33. **Seasonal calendar and demand pattern.** Own monthly sales for three years; peak months; share of annual sales in the top three months; prompts for pre-season actions (marked as the owner's own data, as no outside share was verified).
34. **Back-to-school and sports-season campaign planner.** Target group, price, expected orders, blank need, press hours, deadline; verdict if capacity covers it.
35. **Cash flow forecast, 12 weeks.** Deposits and balances in, blanks and supplies out, market fees, owner pay; flag the week cash falls below a floor.
36. **Owner pay and tax set-aside.** Profit after costs, owner pay target, set-aside percentage (owner setting, "confirm with your adviser"), remaining; verdict "pay yourself / hold".
37. **Break-even and monthly overhead.** Fixed costs (rent, software, insurance, subscriptions, equipment loan) over average margin per item gives units to break even; scenario with 20% fewer orders.
38. **Compare with my own history.** Every monthly report shows this month vs last month vs same month last year for sales, margin, misprint rate, ink per print, blank stock value.
39. **Start-here tab and demo data.** Each workbook: "Start here", clear "replace demo data" steps, no macros, works in Excel and Google Sheets, short help line for one-person start-ups showing which tabs to skip.
40. **Positioning in plain words.** One-line "Why a workbook" block: one flat price, no monthly fee, no per-user fee, your data stays with you, built for blanks-and-press shops (stock, capacity, per-item cost) rather than commercial print.

**Suggested 8 part titles (no overlap):**
1. Pricing and Cost per Item (cost sheets, ink and paper per print, margin and price tests, fee calculator)
2. Quotes, Bulk and Wholesale Orders (quotes, minimums, rush, tiers, wholesale lists, deposits)
3. Order Intake and Proofs (order register, approvals, deposit and balance, customer and repeat register)
4. Production Queue and Press Capacity (queue, capacity, season forecast, misprint and reprint log)
5. Blanks and Supplies Stock (stock by type, size, colour, reorder points, suppliers, dead stock, ink and paper use)
6. Equipment and Upkeep (printers, presses, replacement, return on equipment, maintenance)
7. Markets, Online and Shipping (craft fairs, online sales fees, shipping cost, seasonal campaigns)
8. Money, Pay and Yearly Plan (cash forecast, break-even, owner pay, tax set-aside, year review, compare with own history)

## Product wording rules
- No brand, software, printer-maker, ink-maker, blank-maker, marketplace, platform, bank or company names anywhere in the workbooks, sheet names, help text or listing. Say "an online marketplace", "your own website", "a payment provider", "your supplier".
- No law, regulation, agency, licence, program or standard names. Say "your local rules", "your sales tax / VAT / GST", "confirm with your adviser".
- No claims about trademark or copyright clearance of designs; do not say designs are safe to print. Add only "you are responsible for the right to use the artwork you print" in generic words (no law names).
- No safety guarantees (heat, fumes, food contact, child safety): say "follow your supplier's instructions".
- No invented figures: all prices, margins and rates in the product are demo values the owner replaces; the sheet says so.
- Gender-neutral wording throughout ("the owner", "the customer", "they").
- Use "blank", "transfer", "press", "personalised / personalized" with a note that both spellings are accepted; show UK/EU/AU labels as help text only.
