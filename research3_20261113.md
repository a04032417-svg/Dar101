## Conclusion (honest)

No profession-specific business spreadsheet appeared with a high item review count. The high-review listings found (203, 99, 88) are generic, Etsy-seller or personal-budget products. The profession listings I could read had 0 to 4 reviews. Most profession results are 2026 listings (IDs 45xxxxxxxx), so they are new and have not built up reviews. The market evidence here shows volume in cheap generic and personal sheets ($1 to $17), not in $29 profession suites. It does not show any profession beating the already-built ones.

## Top-3 candidates not in the skip lists (low confidence, no review proof)

1. Reseller (eBay/Poshmark/Depop): appears in TrackerPro's catalog and in many results. Common solo business, strong spreadsheet fit (inventory, cost, profit per item). No review numbers were verified.
2. Booth renter / barber / stylist (chair-rental income, tips, Schedule C): several new listings (4521622872, 4584677274), but no reviews were verified. Close to the already-built hair salon, so overlap risk.
3. Freelancer / self-employed consultant (income, 1099, tax set-aside): the freelancer listing has only 1 review (442-sale shop); a generic income tracker is at 64.2k shop sales. Demand looks real but prices are low ($3 to $9).

Verdict: no Etsy evidence beats the already-built professions. If one more is needed, reseller has the best fit with a large solo-seller base, but this is not proven by reviews.
