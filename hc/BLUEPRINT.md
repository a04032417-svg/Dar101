# Home care agency suite — eight parts (all share hc_core.py: owner-director, scheduler and care coordinator, office manager, part-time recruiter, 2 field supervisors, 10 outside contacts, about 38 caregivers, 54 active clients, 6 areas, about 900 care hours a week, ~2 years of history)

The demo business is "Maple & Ash Home Care" (fictional): a small NON-MEDICAL home care agency run by an owner-director (Marguerite Adeyemi, HC-01) with a scheduler and care coordinator (Tomasz Kowalski, HC-02), an office manager who does billing and payroll (Priyanka Nair, HC-03), a part-time recruiter (Camille Dubois, HC-04) and two field supervisors (Joaquin Ramirez, Amara Diallo). Ten outside contacts: accountant, payroll service, insurance broker, background-check provider, training provider, supplies vendor, marketing helper, landlord, IT support and answering service. About 38 caregivers now (22 hourly, 12 part-time, 4 live-in; 12 left in two years) look after 54 active clients (about three in four private pay; the rest paid by a care insurer or a third-party payer) in six areas (North Hills, Riverside, Old Town, East Park, Lakeside, South Valley). Services are companion care, personal care help, homemaking, respite care, overnight care and live-in days, billed by weekday, evening, weekend, night and holiday band, with a minimum visit length per client. Each part is a standalone workbook that carries its own copies of the reference tables it needs. Charts group by month or by a list or ref column whose values the demo data uses fully (service, band, area, payer, status, source, reason, employment, cycle, weekday, cover type, kind, category); never by a calc column, an id, a client or caregiver name, a quarter or a year list, and never with hundreds of rows as an axis. A table with fewer than 12 rows may have only 3 columns: design for that (Areas, Services, Payers, Credential Types). STRICTLY NON-MEDICAL: the workbook holds service types, hours, dates, rates and amounts only; never a diagnosis, a condition, a medication, a care note, a vital sign or care advice, and no promise about health or safety. No brand, platform, software, bank, insurer or company names and no named law, regulation, agency, licence, programme or standard anywhere ("your local rules", "a third-party payer"). A one-person start-up agency can use most parts (Enquiries, Quotes, Schedule, Visits, Payroll, Invoices, Cash); every help text of those pages says so.
