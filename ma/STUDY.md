# STUDY — independent martial arts school / dojo / academy (market and workflow)

Method: 25 web calls (searches and fetches) on 2026-11-15, building on "Research 14" in research6_20261115.md (not repeated: Payhip retention SOP bundle at 37 USD, Gleantap dashboard existence, financial-model ecosystem). Most numbers below come from software vendors' own blogs, so they are vendor-grade evidence and marked as such. Brand names appear in this study only as research sources; none may reach the product. Anything not verified says NOT verified.

## 1. Real jobs and pain points

**Daily jobs (owner, program director, front desk):** open and check the day's class list by program and rank group; check attendance (hand or kiosk) and mark trial and make-up students; greet and enrol walk-ins and trial students; follow up trial students the same day; answer enquiries (calls, messages, web form); take payments at the desk (gear, testing, drop-ins); note failed payments and ask the family; log owner-reported incidents; cover for an absent instructor; lock-up cash check.
**Weekly jobs:** next week's timetable against instructor availability and floor capacity (two floors, overlapping classes); class headcount per class (full, thin, to merge); trial-to-member follow-up list; "who has not come for two weeks" call list (drop-off risk by attendance); failed and declined payments retry and call list; freeze and cancellation requests; make-up and private lesson bookings; instructor and assistant hours for the pay run; uniform and gear low-stock check; after-school sign-in and pick-up roster; social posts and referral asks; weekly cash check.
**Monthly jobs:** billing run (tuition, family plans, sibling discounts, agreements ending, sign-up fees) and payment-processing fee check; owed versus collected; count of active, frozen, new and lost members (many owners count by hand at month end); retention and cancellations with reason; testing results and new belts to order; instructor pay (per class, per hour, salary, volunteer expenses); gear orders and stock count; event and camp deposits; rent, insurance, software, marketing; profit and loss; owner pay; tax set-aside transfer.
**Seasonal and yearly jobs:** January and September joining peaks and the summer slump; term plan of testing dates (often 2-4 per student per year); summer camp and holiday camp sell-out and staffing; tournament and seminar calendar (entry fees, travel, hotel, profit); belt-test season and black-belt test; price rise and notice to members; agreement renewals (6-12 months) and auto-renew letters; uniform, safety-gear and mat replacement budget; insurance renewal; instructor reviews and promotions into the leadership team; leadership or black-belt club upgrade drives; year-end pack for the accountant; lease and floor review.

**Pain points reported (sources):**
1. Failed payments go unseen for months: an owner quoted by a vendor blog said a payment had not gone through for a year before she noticed; processing fees are hard to total (example check: 412 in fees on 10,000 collected is 4.1%). https://gymdesk.com/blog/signs-you-need-new-gym-management-software (vendor blog quoting owners)
2. Paper or spreadsheets are still used for attendance, belt ranks and member counts ("manual tax"); month-end counts of members are done by hand because the system gives no total; 43.2% of gyms in one platform still mark attendance by hand. same source; https://gymdesk.com/blog/vital-metrics-for-martial-arts-business-owners
3. Low-tier plans lock basic tools (text reminders, waivers) behind higher prices; vendors raise price while the product stays the same ("the best way to grow is just charge your people more", an owner quoted); support tickets unanswered. https://gymdesk.com/blog/signs-you-need-new-gym-management-software (vendor blog, owner quotes)
4. Multi-location or multi-class owners must open three dashboards and add the totals themselves. same source.
5. Software not built for martial arts struggles with ranks, family accounts and kids programs; household records are fragmented. same source; https://blackbeltcrm.com/spreadsheets-vs-software-for-my-student-database (vendor marketing, not documented findings).
6. Warning signs of leaving sit in attendance data but nobody looks: members who come less than once in two weeks cancel at 44.2% against 23.2% for 3+ visits a week (platform data, observational). https://gymdesk.com/blog/vital-metrics-for-martial-arts-business-owners
7. Testing fees: students and parents worry about cumulative fees up to black belt; owners defend fees as covering belts, certificates, breaking boards, visiting examiners; a few owners charge nothing or let students buy their own belt. https://martialtalk.com/threads/belt-fees-promotion-fees.123843/page-3 (owner forum, undated; many posts old).
8. Accounting tools do money well but not students; student tools may lack accounting; one owner said general accounting software has far more features than a small dojo needs; reliability and discontinued-product risk. https://martialtalk.com/threads/financial-software-what-do-you-use-for-your-martial-arts-school.39473/ (thread mostly 2006-2011, old).
9. Front-desk staff end up chasing payments instead of running the desk; the same message is rewritten for every family. https://blackbeltcrm.com/spreadsheets-vs-software-for-my-student-database (vendor)
10. Growth strains old setups (one school growing by about 50 students a year); a second location makes reports harder. Gymdesk blog above.
NOT verified: no readable owner-group posts (Facebook groups, Reddit) were available; owner voice comes from a vendor blog's quotes and one old forum. No spreadsheet-specific complaints from martial-arts owners were found.

Prices below are from a competitor-written comparison (Gymdesk blog, "Martial Arts Billing Software: 7 Platforms Compared for 2026", https://gymdesk.com/blog/martial-arts-billing-software), so the writer sells one of them; treat as indicative, as of 2026.

Reading: a 120-350 member school pays about 100-250 a month plus 2.5-3.5% card processing (vendor figure), or about 1,200-3,000 a year before add-ons. Hidden costs: per-member tier jumps, text messages, branded app, door access, payment processing, migration. A third-party forum thread lists an older Access-based product whose maker closed.
Features offered: recurring billing, failed-payment retries (about 6 in 10 recovered), family billing, attendance check-in, rank and belt tracking, testing events, trial and lead pipeline, waivers, text and email, point of sale, reports.
User complaints (Capterra/G2 reviews could not be fetched: NOT verified; the following come from vendor blogs quoting owners): failed payments not flagged, price rises, features gated, slow support, belt and family handling generic, reports too thin or too many, dashboards per location.
**What a spreadsheet must do better:** one flat 29; no member-count tier; the owner sees profit by program, class and instructor (software shows member counts and billing, not whether a class pays); testing, gear and event profit in the same file; instructor pay per class with real cost per class; drop-off risk ranked from attendance the owner pastes in; cash and owner-pay plan; works for a solo one-floor school; owner keeps the data. Must not pretend to take payments.

## 6. Gaps and improvements for the blueprint
Rule: every feature below is a real job with a calculation; all targets are editable settings; no brand, law, body or style names.

1. **Class profit finder:** per class: attendees x average paid per member share, minus instructor pay, floor cost per hour; verdict "keep / merge / grow / drop"; ranked list of worst five classes.
2. **Class headcount versus capacity** by weekday and program: fill rate, over-capacity flag, thin-class flag; "merge candidates" list.
3. **Floor-hours planner** for one or two floors: hours booked by program, free hours, overlaps, rent per used hour; verdict for adding a class.
4. **Timetable builder by rank group** with instructor assignment and conflict check (instructor in two places, floor double-booked).
5. **Trial and intro-offer funnel:** enquiries, booked trials, attended, joined, with conversion at each step by source and by program; compare with own history and the 73% / 20-40% reference as editable.
6. **Trial follow-up clock:** days since trial, next action, hot list ranked by days left in the 10-day window; verdict "call today".
7. **Intro-offer test:** cost of discount versus extra joiners (break-even conversion), verdict "worth it / not worth it".
8. **Lead source value:** value per enquiry and per joined member by source (referral, web, leaflet, school visit, event); keep/drop decision; cost to win a member versus lifetime value.
9. **Referral reward tool:** reward cost versus expected members and months kept; best reward size.
10. **Member register with agreement dates:** start, term, renewal due, price, program, family link; renewals due in 30/60/90 days list with revenue at stake.
11. **Family plan and discount calculator:** sibling rate, family cap, revenue lost to discounts by family; verdict "discount costs 6% of tuition".
12. **Monthly billing check:** expected tuition by member and family versus collected; owed, collected, never charged, declined (the 8% gap) as four lines; flag members with no charge.
13. **Failed-payment call list:** days past due, amount, attendance last 30 days, ranked by risk and value; retry log and outcome; share recovered.
14. **Processing fee check:** fees over collected, effective rate, target, cost per year; verdict on surcharge or plan change.
15. **Freeze register:** freeze reason, start, end date, fee, revenue paused; "freezes ending next month" list; share frozen versus cancelled.
16. **Cancellation log with reason and tenure:** reasons ranked, tenure at leaving (30/90/180 days), by program and instructor; "lost revenue per month" figure.
17. **Drop-off risk list:** pasted attendance per member, visits last 4 weeks versus own norm, ranked list with action; uses the attendance bands (<0.5, 1, 2, 3+ visits a week) with editable cut-offs.
18. **Retention and churn by cohort:** join month cohorts, share still active at 3, 6, 12 months; compare with own history.
19. **Monthly member movement:** opening, new, returned, frozen, lost, closing; ties to billing count (answers the hand-count at month end).
20. **Rank register and eligibility:** each member's belt, date of last promotion, classes since, required classes and months; "eligible this cycle" list and "short by n classes" list (rules set by the owner).
21. **Testing event planner:** candidates, fees, belts and certificates to order, examiner or assistant cost, room hire; profit per testing day and per candidate; verdict if fees cover cost.
22. **Testing fee ladder tool:** fee by rank, cumulative cost to black belt shown to the parent, tests per year; "is my fee fair" check against cost per test (belt, certificate, time).
23. **Time-in-rank and pace check:** average months per rank by program, students stuck beyond norm (at risk of quitting).
24. **Promotion revenue and gear tie:** each promotion creates a belt and stripe order list; promotion-to-stock link so nothing is ordered twice.
25. **Leadership / black-belt club upgrade tool:** who qualifies, upgrade price, extra revenue per month, cost of perks; payback and verdict.
26. **Uniform and gear stock:** item, size, cost, price, on hand, reorder point, sold, margin per item and per month; "reorder now" list; dead stock list; gear margin versus markup target.
27. **Pro-shop profit check:** revenue, cost of goods, margin by item and category; stock value tied up; verdict on keeping a slow line.
28. **Gear bundle and starter-kit pricing:** kit cost versus price, effect on joiners; start-pack margin.
29. **Instructor pay calculator:** per class, per hour, salary and volunteer expense; pay by month; paid classes, assistant hours, overtime check; cost per class and per attendee.
30. **Instructor and assistant scorecard:** classes taught, average headcount, retention of their students, revenue per teaching hour, pay share; compared with own history; never ranks people by anything but business numbers.
31. **Hire or promote test:** expected extra classes, headcount, pay; break-even members for adding an instructor; verdict.
32. **After-school program model:** children per day, fee per child, pick-up costs (vehicle, driver, fuel), staff ratio, break-even children, profit per child and per week; enrolment roster and sign-out log as owner records.
33. **Camp and holiday program planner:** weekly fee, campers, staff, ratio, extras, profit per week and per camper; sell-out forecast; early-bird versus full price.
34. **Tournament and seminar profit:** entry fees collected, owed to organiser, travel and hotel, coaches, parents' share; profit per event; "run it again?" verdict; guest instructor split.
35. **Private lesson tracker:** coach, length, price, paid or package, coach pay, margin per hour; share of coaches' time; unused packages liability.
36. **Birthday party planner and profit:** package price, helpers, food, cost, margin per party; floor-time cost; bookings calendar; conversion of guests to trials.
37. **Revenue by stream and per member:** tuition, sign-up, testing, gear, privates, events, camps, after-school, parties; share and monthly trend; revenue per member versus the 66-102 reference range as editable.
38. **Monthly profit and loss with owner pay and tax set-aside:** all costs (rent share, insurance, processing, software, marketing) with target bands; owner pay as a line; cash cushion in days and months; scenario test for losing 5% of members or raising price 5%.
39. **Break-even and price-rise test:** members needed to break even, effect of price rise given expected cancellations, family-plan effects; verdict "raise / hold" with the number of leavers that cancels the gain.
40. **Solo-school and start-here pack:** a "Solo mode" that hides instructor and floor sections, a start tab with demo data replace steps, "sample plus blank" files, no macros, works in Excel and Google Sheets, and a one-line why-a-workbook block (flat price, no member tiers, your data stays with you, reports for owners: class profit, drop-off list, testing profit). Every tax cell says "confirm with your adviser".

## Product wording rules
- No brand, software, platform, bank or company names anywhere in the product (this study's names are research sources only).
- No law, regulation, agency, licence, governing-body, federation or standard names; say "your local rules", "your association" (only as a generic phrase), "your insurance renewal".
- No medical, injury-treatment or self-defence-outcome claims; injury notes are owner-reported incidents only; say "ask a health professional".
- No style-lineage, federation, gear or uniform brand names; belt colours and rank names are owner-typed text, not shipped as a named system.
- Child-safe, gender-neutral wording; members are "students" or "members"; guardians as "parent or guardian"; no personal child details beyond what the owner already holds; sample data uses made-up names.
- No promise of results (retention, income, conversion); benchmarks shown as "reference ranges, change them to your own".
- Accept regional words (dojo, academy, club, grading, kit, direct debit) as labels without naming bodies or products.
