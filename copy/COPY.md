# Sales copy standard for Dar Templates suites (2026-11-12, after the owner: «العناوين ضعيفة وغير جذابة والوصف فاشل»)

The buyer is a busy professional. Every text must answer, in this order: what do I GET OUT of it (result), is it FOR ME, what EXACTLY do I receive, is it EASY, why buy NOW.

## Fields and rules
- seo_title (≤70; it is also the Whop and Shopify product title): RESULT + product type + profession. Never a list of features or keywords.
  Good: "Farm Management System in Excel – Know Your Profit per Field". Bad: "Farm Excel Templates – Crops, Livestock, Machinery, Finance".
- headline (≤80): the strongest promise in plain words, with a concrete pain it removes.
- title (≤140, marketplace title): result first, then 3–5 searchable modules, end with "15 Languages".
- seo_description (≤160): result + what is inside + 15 languages.
- description: 4 short blocks separated by blank lines:
  1. Hook (2 sentences): the result the buyer gets + the costly mistake it prevents. Speak to "you".
  2. For whom (1 sentence): the people and business sizes it fits.
  3. What you get: one line per item, each starting with "✓ " — 8 connected workbooks (name them), {pages} ready pages (dashboards, calculators, printable forms, checklists, ready messages), 15 languages each blank + filled sample, standard xlsx files for Microsoft Excel and LibreOffice, no macros, no subscription.
  4. How it works (3 steps) + closing line "One payment, instant download."
  The first ~1,300 characters must stand alone (Whop cuts at 1,500).
- features (12): each a RESULT the buyer gets ("Know…", "See…", "Never miss…", "Stop…", "Get…"), ≤ 90 characters, concrete.
- faq (6): software, fits small/large, replace sample data, currency/units, where to start, macros/subscription/delivery.
- tags (13, ≤20 chars): what buyers type.
- Native languages: write as a native copywriter of that market would, same promises, not word for word; gender-neutral; never the word "compatible", no Excel versions, no brand names except Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice and WhatsApp.
- No invented facts: numbers only from the product (workbooks, pages, languages, sample data).
