## Lessons from parts 1-4 of the Online Seller suite (carry over)
- List keys must be ≤21 characters: ho_core pairs() keys are already short, but your own lists can give longer keys (a recipe name, a task, a kind); truncate with the P() helper (see cc4_lists.py).

- COUNTIFS blank-row lesson: COUNTIFS and SUMIFS over a whole prepared range count the empty prepared rows too when a criterion matches blanks (for example "<>" or ""), and a count of "chores without an owner", "items without a receipt" or "tasks not done" must test an input column that is filled on real rows (require the id column to be non-blank, for example COUNTIFS({range:t.id},"<>",{range:t.receipt_date},"")). Never count blank rows as open items.
- Compare only with complete periods: a "this month against last month" or "this year against last year" comparison must use complete periods on both sides (or the same number of days to date on both sides); never compare a month to date with a full month, and never a partial year with a full year. For "this month to date" use the same number of days to date on both sides; fairness and history comparisons use this month against the 3 complete months before it.
- CI-only date lesson: a date output that falls back to 99999999 (or any large number) on the EMPTY template fails the CI verify, because the blank copy has no rows. Write "next due", "nearest use-by", "next expiry" and "next renewal" outputs as IFERROR(SMALL(range,COUNTIF(range,"<"&today)+1),"") (the smallest date on or after today, blank when there is none) and guard every days-until with IF(date="","",date-today). The same holds for ranked lists: no MIN or SMALL over an empty range without IFERROR.

## Lessons from the Household Finance suite and the Home Management foundation (2026-11-18)
- Ranked and helper tables: rows of a ranked list (use first, most overdue, cook this first, ranked renewals) or of a helper table that have no key (no item, no task, no recipe) must be blank in EVERY calculated column: never show numbers, zeros, flags or "ok" on an empty row. Test the key first (IF({col:item}="","",...)) in every calc column of the table, and make a KPI or chart that reads the table ignore the blank rows (COUNTIFS with the key column "<>").
