# STUDY — home management (a single adult, a couple or a family running its HOME: meals, chores, upkeep, things, calendar, pets, vehicles, papers, projects): market and workflow

Method: 25 web calls that ran (10 searches, 15 fetches) on 2026-11-18. Builds on Research 29 and 30 in research6_20261115.md (not repeated: homeschool and home-organizer leads, unlabeled counts, "NOT VERIFIED"). The prompt names a "Research 31" section; it does not exist in that file (the file ends at Research 30), so nothing was taken from it. Evidence is thin in places and says so; findniche returned no etsy-product page for any home-management query, so listing sales are NOT verified except where an Etsy page shows an item count. Brand names appear in this study only as research sources; none may reach the product. This is not a household money suite (budget, bills, debt, savings, net worth belong to another suite) and not a cleaning-business suite: money appears only as simple cost lines of a project or a repair.

## 1. Real jobs and pain points

**Daily:** what is for dinner tonight and is anything thawing or to be used first; dishes, wipe-down, tidy, laundry load of the day; who does today's chores; today's school run, activities and carpool; feed and walk pets (date or tick only); mark a chore done and, for children, add points.
**Weekly:** plan 5-7 dinners; turn the plan into a grocery list (recipes to ingredients, minus what the pantry and freezer already hold, grouped by aisle or shop section); shop; use leftovers; cleaning routine by room; rotate chores between household members and check the load is fair; laundry rota and bedding change; bin day (UK/AU) or trash and recycling day; what is on this week (calendar, visitors, appointments); children's chore chart and points total; pantry glance: what expires soon.
**Monthly:** pantry and freezer check with use-by dates and "what to use first"; filters and consumables (water filter, vacuum bags, batteries, light bulbs, pet food stock); alarm test; deep-clean list by room; maintenance tasks due this month and overdue ones; birthdays and gifts coming in the next 60 days; documents and renewals due in the next 90 days; project check (on track or late).
**Seasonal (spring, summer, autumn/fall, winter):** home maintenance by season (gutters, heating and cooling service, boiler service, seasonal checks of roof, windows, outdoor taps, garden, chimney); spring clean; wardrobe swap; school terms and half-term/holiday blocks; holiday hosting; garden planting and harvest; winter prep.
**Yearly:** appliance and system service; warranty end dates; vehicle annual test, registration and insurance renewal dates; policy, contract and subscription renewals; passport and licence expiry; vet and grooming dates; annual declutter and donation round; year review of maintenance done and repairs made; next year's calendar of school dates and birthdays.
**Life events:** moving (packing by room, change-of-address list, utilities switched, key dates, box labels, overlap days); renovation (rooms, tasks, trades called in generic words, quotes, simple cost lines, dates); new baby or new pet (stock, dates); hosting a holiday or party (guest list, menu, shopping, tasks by day, timeline from "-14 days" to the day); visitors and a house-sitter (instructions page, contacts, where things are); decluttering by room with keep / donate / sell / bin and a donation log.
**Other jobs the buyer names:** home inventory with purchase date, receipt date, warranty end, manual location and serial number; repairs log with date, what, who was called (a trade in generic words), cost line, "fixed" status; emergency and household contacts; documents checklist; gift ideas with names and dates only; leftovers and freezer log; school-year calendar; carpool rota.

**Pain points reported (sources):**
1. **Chore apps do not fix fairness; they can add work.** An article on chore apps quotes users: an app "doesn't solve the problem: that you're nagging someone else or parenting your partner"; apps "often require women, especially mothers, to manage the technology and assign tasks"; "People get defensive when they are notified of ways they are not being equal partners"; a points-based app reportedly moved one household from about 90-10 to 60-40 and helped a partner "notice that so many more chores exist than just sweeping, vacuuming, cooking, and dishes". Source: https://www.technologyreview.com/2022/05/10/1051954/chore-apps/amp/ . Reading: a visible, neutral fairness check (load per person, with the hidden jobs listed) is the need; the tool must not nag.
2. **Meal-planning app upsells and gaps.** One reviewer of a food app: it "prompted for an immediate upsell" before testing; photo recipe scanning and cook mode are behind the paywall; "The suggested meal plans didn't reflect my dietary preferences"; only one grocery store can be set on the shopping list; "No way was found to track leftovers, frozen meals, or batch cooking"; recipes organised only by collections. Prices on the page: $59.99/year or $6.99/month (food app); $5.95/month or $49/year (a recipe-planner app, 14-day trial). One reviewer, one page: https://www.plantoeat.com/?p=9210 . Reading: leftovers, freezer and "use first" tracking are open gaps.
3. **Food gets wasted without a plan.** A study of about 4,000 US households found the average household wastes 31.9% of the food it acquires (range: most households 20-50%; the least wasteful 8.7%); households using a shopping list waste less; planning is named as a key factor; some pack sizes lead to waste in smaller households. Study published 2020 in an economics journal, reported by a trade site: https://www.foodlogistics.com/sustainability/press-release/21113800/study-suggests-us-households-waste-nearly-onethird-of-food . Medium strength; US only; not to be quoted as a promise.
4. **Family organizer apps glitch and lack small things.** Cozi app-store review analysis: "The app and website glitch a lot"; "gets a little wonky when making lots of adjustments to lists"; a "Terrible update" that made text hard to read; users want colour-coded appointments and detail on who changed what and when; account management limits and no search function are named. Praise: shared calendars and shopping lists. No counts given (sentiment breakdown paid): https://kimola.com/reports/explore-in-depth-user-feedback-analysis-on-cozi-family-organizer-app-store-us-148390 . NOT verified: Trustpilot pages for the same app were listed in search but not read.
5. **Home-maintenance lists are generic and static.** The long public checklists give only seasonal groupings ("Clean your gutters and downspouts" in spring/summer and again fall/winter; "Test smoke alarms ... " monthly; "Change the batteries" twice a year) with no numeric intervals and no "next due" per household: https://myhome.freddiemac.com/blog/homeownership/20211001-home-maintenance . A paid tracker answers this with "auto-calculated Next Due dates and Status" and a service and contractor log (see section 3), which shows the job is wanted.
6. **Single-sheet kits cover one slice.** A cleaning tracker has one Cleaning Log tab; a family chore chart has two tabs (Instructions, Chore Chart); a free chore sheet is "a weekly template only", progress tracking manual. Nothing found links meals, chores, upkeep, inventory, calendar, pets, vehicles, papers and projects (section 3).
7. **Compatibility limits are a complaint-in-waiting.** One cleaning tracker says its Excel version "requires the latest Office 365 desktop app"; a maintenance tracker "works ... but not the Excel mobile app or Apple Numbers"; a household command center listing contradicts itself about its file type ("1 Excel file (.xlsx, 7 sheets)" versus "1 PDF, 1 other file"). The suite must say plainly which programs it works in and open without add-ins.
8. NOT verified: owner-written forum threads (Reddit and others) on chore disputes, meal-plan fatigue or lost warranties; the one forum search returned apps, a hackathon page and app-store pages, not readable threads. The needs above are taken from the article, app reviews and the structure of the jobs.

## 6. Gaps and improvements for the blueprint

(Most valuable first. Every item is a real job with the calculation it needs. Keep all wording generic.)

1. **Dinners from the pantry: what to cook first.** List of recipes (ingredients with quantities) scored against pantry and freezer stock; each recipe gets "% of ingredients on hand" and "uses items expiring soonest"; ranked list ends with a verdict "cook this first".
2. **Weekly meal plan to grocery list.** 7-day grid (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack optional), servings scaled to the household size of that day; list = needed minus on hand, rounded to pack sizes typed by the user, grouped by section and by shop (several shops, fixing the one-store limit).
3. **Week with one adult away / guests staying.** Scenario switch: set adults and children at home per day; servings, grocery list and chore load recalculate; shows the change in items and portions.
4. **Pantry and freezer stock with use-by.** Item, location, quantity, unit, date added, use-by or best-before typed from the label; status "ok / use soon (within X days, set in Settings) / past date"; "use first" ranked list; stock below a minimum goes to the grocery list.
5. **Leftovers and freezer log.** Dish, date cooked, portions left, move-to-freezer date, "eat by" typed; list ranked by date; portions left feed the meal plan (the gap the app reviews name).
6. **Grocery list history.** Items bought per week with a typed price line (optional), staples that recur, "always runs out" ranking by how many weeks an item appears; compare with own history, no price advice.
7. **Cleaning routine by room and frequency.** Task per room with frequency (daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, yearly), last done date, next due date, overdue days; weekly plan generated and ranked by most overdue.
8. **Chore rotation generator.** Members, tasks, effort points per task (typed), days available; the tool assigns by rotation or by balancing points so each person's total is close; a "swap" cell for a week.
9. **Fairness check of chore load per person.** Points and minutes per member per week and month, share of total, gap to the equal share, and a plain verdict ("adult 1 carries 62% this month"); includes a list of hidden tasks (planning, booking, remembering) with typed owner, so unseen work is visible.
10. **Chore week with one person away or ill-day-off.** Remove a member for chosen days; tasks redistribute and the new shares show; shows which tasks become unowned.
11. **Children's chores and points.** Child 1..n, tasks with points, week grid, points earned, reward ladder (typed rewards at typed point levels), points balance carried forward; no money logic beyond an optional "allowance value per point" line.
12. **Chore completion history.** Weekly done / missed per member, streaks, and "compare with own history" (this month vs last 3 months).
13. **Laundry rota.** Loads per week by type (colours, whites, bedding, towels), who, day; bedding and towel change next-due dates; the week's busiest laundry day.
14. **Home maintenance master schedule.** Task, area, season, interval (typed days/months), last done, auto next due, status (ok / due soon / overdue), and a ranked "most overdue first" list; default intervals shown as "typical, check the manual".
15. **Maintenance calendar by season and next 12 months.** Every task's next due date projected across the year (repeat dates), a month-by-month count, and the busiest month highlighted.
16. **Filters and consumables.** Item, size or code (typed), where used, change interval, last changed, units in stock; next change date and "reorder before" date; low-stock list.
17. **Appliance and system service history.** Per appliance or system: service dates, who (a trade in generic words), cost line, next service due; "service overdue" list; yearly service cost per item.
18. **Repairs log and the trades called.** Date, area, issue (short), trade called (generic), quote, cost, status (open / waiting / fixed), days open; open-repairs list ranked by days; total by room and by year; "repeat repairs" flag (same area twice in 12 months).
19. **Trades and contacts directory.** Trade type, name, phone, last used date, cost last time (typed), "would use again" yes/no; links to the repair log.
20. **Home inventory with warranty countdown.** Item, room, purchase date, price, serial, receipt date, manual location, warranty end typed; days left; ranked "warranties ending in 90 days"; "expired" list.
21. **Where is the manual / receipt.** Search cell: type an item name, see its room, serial, receipt date, manual and receipt location; total value by room (a sum of typed prices).
22. **Replace-or-repair view.** For one item: age (from purchase date), repairs logged, repair cost total vs a typed replacement price; ratio and a plain verdict against the household's own rule (a typed percentage, default labelled "your rule").
23. **Family calendar with conflict finder.** Events per member with date, start and end times; the tool lists overlaps per person and double-booked carpools ("child 1 and child 2 at different places 17:00-18:00 and one adult"); count of conflicts this week.
24. **Busiest week ahead.** Count of events and tasks per week for the next 12 weeks; busiest and quietest weeks; a verdict "week 14 has 19 items".
25. **School terms and holiday blocks.** Term start and end, half-term / break dates typed; days to the next break; days of school left; child-care gap days (days off with no activity typed).
26. **Kids' activities and carpool rota.** Activity, day, time, place (generic), who drives; weekly driving load per adult (count and minutes), and a fairness line like the chore check.
27. **Birthdays and gifts list.** Name, date, age turning (if year typed), gift idea, gift status (idea / bought / wrapped / given), cost line; next 60 days list; names and dates only.
28. **Holidays and visitors.** Visitor name, arrival and leave dates, rooms and beds used, meals to add to the meal plan, tasks before arrival; clash check if two stays overlap or beds are over capacity.
29. **Pets care schedule.** Per pet: care tasks with interval, last done, next due (grooming, vet visit as a date only, tablets or treatments NOT included); food stock in days left from a typed daily amount; "reorder date".
30. **Vehicle service and renewal dates.** Per vehicle: service, tyres, annual test (generic), registration and insurance renewal dates typed; days left; ranked next-90-days list; mileage typed to estimate next service by typed interval.
31. **Documents and renewals checklist.** Passports, licences, policies, contracts (generic words): holder, expiry or renewal date, where kept, "action needed" lead time typed (default 60 days); ranked by days left; family-wide list per member.
32. **Renewals in the next 90 days, all in one.** One dashboard pulls dates from warranties, vehicles, documents, pets, filters and contracts: sorted list "what is coming" with type, owner and days left; count per month.
33. **Emergency and household contacts + sitter sheet.** Printable one page: adults and children's first names, contact numbers typed, school hours, house routines (bin day, feed times), where things are, who to call (generic trades); no health details.
34. **Guest and hosting plan.** Event, date, guests with RSVP status and "preference / avoid" short label, menu, shopping list quantities by guest count, task list counted back from the date; "days left, tasks open, shopping not bought" verdict.
35. **Project tracker (move, renovation, garden, party).** Tasks with owner, start, due, status, dependency (done before X), and simple cost line (estimate, actual, paid); percent complete, days late, cost vs estimate; verdict "on track / at risk / late".
36. **Move date moved by two weeks.** Scenario: change the move date by a typed number of days; every dated task, notice, booking and overlap day shifts; shows tasks that now fall before today and extra overlap days.
37. **Moving checklist by room and countdown.** Packing by room (boxes counted, label, packed yes/no), change-of-address list by organisation type (generic), "8 weeks to 1 day before" countdown, boxes left.
38. **Decluttering and donations.** Area, item or category, decision (keep / donate / sell / bin), count, date; progress by area; donation log with date and item count; "areas not started" list.
39. **Year review of the home.** Maintenance tasks done vs planned, repairs count and cost lines, chores done by member, projects finished, warranties ended, meals planned vs cooked; compared with last year if typed; ends with three typed "next year" lines.
40. **Start here, shared Settings and verdict wording in every workbook.** Replace demo data, weekly routine (Sunday: plan meals, check list, rotate chores, glance at calendar), monthly routine (pantry, maintenance, renewals), household members (adult 1, adult 2, child 1...), rooms, areas, "due soon" window days, currency symbol, week start day, date format (day/month or month/day), and measurement units (metric or imperial); the same names and checks across all eight workbooks; every verdict labelled "typical, check the manufacturer's instructions and your local rules".

**Suggested 8 part titles (no overlap):**
1. Meal Plan and Grocery List (recipes, week grid, scaling, shopping list by shop)
2. Pantry, Freezer and Leftovers (stock, use-by, use-first ranking, minimum stock, "cook this first")
3. Cleaning, Laundry and Chore Rotation (routine by room, rotation, children's points, fairness check, away-week test)
4. Home Maintenance and Repairs (master schedule, seasons, filters, appliance service, repairs log, trades)
5. Home Inventory and Warranties (items, receipts, manuals, serials, warranty countdown, repair-or-replace view)
6. Family Calendar, Pets and Vehicles (events, school terms, activities, carpools, conflict finder, birthdays, visitors, pet and vehicle schedules)
7. Documents, Contacts and Renewals (documents checklist, contacts, sitter sheet, the 90-day renewals dashboard)
8. Projects, Hosting and Declutter (move, renovation, garden, party, spring clean, decluttering and donations, year review of the home)
Notes on links: Part 1 and Part 2 share the stock list; Part 6 feeds the busiest-week count and Part 3 reads away days; Part 7's renewals dashboard reads dates from Parts 4, 5 and 6; the year review in Part 8 reads from all others. Shared Settings list the same household members and rooms in all eight.

## Product wording rules

- No brand, app, store, supermarket, appliance maker, retailer, service or company names anywhere in the product.
- No law, regulation, agency, licence, code or standard names; say "check your local rules" and "check the manufacturer's instructions".
- No health, nutrition, diet, calorie, allergy-treatment, medical or veterinary advice and no health details: food notes only as a short "preference" or "avoid" label; pets and people get dates, tasks and stock, never symptoms or treatments.
- No safety promises: maintenance intervals, alarm tests, shelf-life and freezer times are "typical, check the manual / check the label", never a rule or a guarantee.
- No promise of saved time, money, less food waste or results; use-by, due dates, warranty days and ranks are "calculated from your entries".
- No app, sync, reminder-notification or automatic claims: the workbook takes typed or pasted data and shows what is due when opened.
- Gender-neutral wording: adult 1 and adult 2, household member, child 1.
- Children appear as first names, school year and activity times only.
