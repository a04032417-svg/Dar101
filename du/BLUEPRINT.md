# Doula Business suite — eight parts (all share du_core.py: an owner-doula who also takes clients, 6 contracted doulas, 2 backup doulas on a shared on-call rota, a part-time admin, 6 outside contacts, 2 service areas, about 70 birth clients and 45 postpartum clients a year, ~2 years of history)

The demo business is "Willowmere Doula Collective" (fictional): a small doula agency run by an owner-doula (Amara Okonkwo, DU-01) who also takes clients. Six contracted doulas (DU-02..DU-07: Lucia Ferreira and Priya Raman for birth, Katarzyna Nowak and Fatou Diop for postpartum, Mei-Ling Chen and Sven Lindqvist for both) are paid per client or per hour with an agency split. Two backup doulas (Yasmin Haddad DU-08, Tomas Novak DU-09) share an on-call rota and are paid a weekly stipend plus a flat call-out. A part-time admin (Henrik Solberg, DU-10) does the office work. Six outside contacts (DU-11..DU-16) are an accountant, a class venue, a photographer, a support professional (a generic referral contact), a care-provider office and an insurer. There are 2 service areas (Northgate, Southbank). About 272 clients appear over ~2 years plus the next 8 months of booked due dates; about 70 are birth clients and 45 postpartum clients a year (some buy a combined package). Packages are Birth Essential, Plus and Premier, postpartum hour packs (40, 80, 120 hours and a 60-hour night pack), three combined packages and five add-ons (an extra prenatal visit, a class seat, a photography referral, a support referral note, a debrief visit). Each client has an estimated due date and an on-call window around it (default 14 days before to 14 days after). Prenatal and postpartum visits are logged with hours and mileage, births attended as times and hours only. The agency also runs classes and workshops, tracks referral sources (care-provider offices, past clients, online), reviews, and training and renewal dates. A solo doula can use most parts (one doula, no split, no backup rota: say so in each help text). The business is strictly non-medical: dates, hours, packages, money, capacity and contact notes only. Every number is relative to the build day and comes from du_core.
