# Short-term rental host suite — eight parts (all share sr_core.py: owner host, co-host, 4 cleaners, 2 handymen, 12 units, 3 booking channels plus direct, ~1,150 bookings over ~2 years)

The demo business is "Harbor and Pine Stays" (fictional): a host running 12 furnished units in 4 cities (7 own or leased units, 5 managed for 3 other owners). Each part is a standalone workbook that carries its own copies of the reference tables it needs. Charts group by month or by a list column and show only the first ~20 values of a reference column: design every chart for that (months, unit type, city, channel, status, season, cleaner, topic; never 12 units or 1,150 bookings as an axis unless the blueprint says so: 12 units fit). Charts cannot group by a calc column: chart by input list or ref columns and by month. A table with fewer than 12 rows may have only 3 columns: design for that (Owners, Channels, Cities, Seasons). NO platform brand names anywhere: say "booking platform", "channel 1/2/3" or "Platform A/B/C"; WhatsApp is the only brand allowed, and only in the engine's send links, never in message text.
