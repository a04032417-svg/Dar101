## Lessons from earlier suites (apply them, the build rejects these)
- Keep list keys and table hints within the length limits.
- A checklist's start column must be an INPUT date column, never a calc column.
- Calc status columns must never be blank on demo rows.
- What-if grids need numeric inputs (not text or ids).
- Sheet names unique inside the workbook.
- Charts and breakdowns group by list or month columns (the engine only takes the first 12-20 values of a ref column): never chart bookings or guests one by one; chart by month, status, channel, city, unit type, season, cleaner, topic or category (12 units fit as an axis only where the blueprint says so).
- Charts cannot group by a calc column: chart by input list or ref columns and by month.
- Demo dates spread over the last 12-24 months for every monthly chart (no empty months at the start of the year).
- KPIs "this month" and "last 30 days" must not be 0 on the demo: sr_core guarantees bookings, check-ins, payouts, turnovers and reviews in the current month and the last 30 days; when you thin rows with `n=` keep the recent ones, and check every "this month" KPI shows a value above 0 on the built workbook.
- No platform brand names anywhere (not in a table, list, message, document, hint, listing or competitor entry): "booking platform", "Platform A/B/C", "channel 1/2/3" or "Direct". WhatsApp is the only brand allowed, only in the engine's send links.
- Optional date columns (paid on, done on, cancelled on, reply on, released on, follow-up date, refund date) must show blank, never a zero date like 1899-12-30: wrap every lookup and calc that returns such a date with IF(x=0,"",x). The sr_core generators return '' for a blank date; keep it blank.
- sr_core has one owner host, one co-host, four cleaners, two handypeople and four outside contacts: where a list or chart needs 3+ people use `team` (12) or `CLEANERS`.
- Money: one currency; amounts realistic for the business in sr_core (12 units, about 1,150 bookings over about 2 years; nightly rate 65 to 280; cleaning fee 25 to 95; platform fee 12 to 15%, direct 3%; local tax 4 to 6% of room revenue; management fee 18 to 20%; co-host fee 10% of net revenue; cleaner pay 17 an hour; owner draw 7,000 a month; cash about 9,000 to 75,000 and always positive).
- Units: nights, guests, hours, weeks and percent stay plain labels; no currency symbols. Shares in sr_core (fee_pct, tax_pct, mgmt_pct, occupancy) are 0..1 fractions, so 0.15 = 15% and they suit pct columns; a rating is 1 to 5.
- Links between tables hold the NAME (a `ref` column) or the id (booking BK-0001, unit UN-01); keep a person, guest, unit, owner and cleaner spelled exactly as in sr_core so the parts agree.
- Story rules (ids depend on the build date: always take them from sr_core.story(), never hard-code them): story()['unit_losing'] (a studio) loses money over 12 months after fees and cleaning; story()['missing_payout'] (a Platform B payout) is 3+ weeks past its expected date and unpaid; story()['pending_claim'] is a damage claim of 640 pending; story()['low_review_unit'] has a 90-day review average near 3; story()['overbooked_cleaner'] has 5 turnovers and 15 hours on story()['overbooked_date']; story()['pricing_unit'] is sold out in the local festival week story()['pricing_week'] at the base rate; the season peaks in July and August; no demo row is dated after TODAY unless it is a check-in, check-out, an upcoming booking, a payout date, a due date, a plan or a pricing week.
- Every part-writer verifies validate (0 errors) and build (ok true, warnings []) before reporting; a file that was not built is not finished.

## Lesson from the Online Seller suite (2026-11-13)
