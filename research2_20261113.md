## Caveat
No candidate profession showed strong listing-level evidence. Most niche listings in 2026 are new (0-1 reviews). The ranking below is the best of weak evidence, not proof.

## Top 3 ranking
1. Short-term rental / Airbnb host - the most listings by distinct sellers in search (20+ results); best verified item 11 reviews, shop 2.2k sales (SimStudioSheets, close to the property manager niche already built), plus 151-sale specialist shop. Frame it as host P&L, occupancy, platform fees, cleaning, owner statements.
2. Home care / caregiver agency - the only niche with a high shop volume (13.9k and 9.4k sales) and 16 item reviews, but those listings are low-priced ($3-6) family-caregiver logs, not agency finance. Needs a build-for-owner angle (payroll, clients, shifts, billing); evidence is partial.
3. Print-on-demand seller - 38 item reviews, the highest item count in a niche, but only $0.99, a 120-sale shop, and overlap with the already-built e-commerce/Etsy seller. Consider a reseller/POD suite only if Etsy-seller overlap is acceptable; otherwise real estate investor / flipper (weak: shop 17 sales) is the next fallback.
