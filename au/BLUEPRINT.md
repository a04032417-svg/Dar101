# Author and Self-Publisher Business suite — eight parts (all share au_core.py: an author-publisher who writes under two pen names, a part-time virtual assistant, freelancers paid per job, 22 titles in 68 editions, five online stores plus a direct store, about 2 years of history)

The demo business is "Lanternfield Press" (fictional): the one-person imprint of an author-publisher (Noor Castellan, AU-01) who writes under two pen names, Tamsin Hartwell (mysteries) and Corvin Ashdale (fantasy). A part-time virtual assistant (Dario Lindqvist, AU-02) and freelancers paid per job (two editors AU-03 and AU-04, a cover designer AU-05, a formatter AU-06, two narrators AU-07 and AU-08, a translator AU-09, an ad specialist AU-14, a web developer AU-15, a launch coordinator AU-16) work with an accountant (AU-10), a printer for author copies (AU-11), an event organiser (AU-12) and a rights agent (AU-13): 16 contacts in all. The catalogue is 22 titles (TL-001..TL-022): four series (Lantern Bay Mysteries 6 books, Quiet Hollow Cases 4, The Ember Crown 5, Tidebound Realms 3), two standalones and two box sets, in 68 editions (ED-0001..) in ebook, paperback, hardcover (some), large print (some) and audiobook (some). Sales run through five online stores in generic words (an ebook store that also pays a page-read income line, a print-on-demand service, an audiobook store, a library and lending channel, a wide-distribution service) and the author's own direct store, in six currencies and eight countries, with royalty payments 45 to 90 days after month end (the direct store pays out in a week). Launches use pre-orders, reviewer copies and newsletter pushes; ads run on three generic ad platforms (store ads, social ads, search ads); price promotions lift single weeks; events and fairs sell hand-sold author copies; rights deals (translation, audio) are kept as status and amounts only. A first-time author with 1 to 3 books uses most parts with a few rows (every part says so in its help texts); a small press that manages several authors reads the pen-name columns as author columns (Part 8 prints a statement per author). Every number is relative to the build day and comes from au_core.
