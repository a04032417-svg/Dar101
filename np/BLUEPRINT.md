# Nonprofit Organization suite — eight parts (all share np_core.py: an executive director, a development manager, a program manager, a volunteer coordinator, three part-time program staff and a part-time bookkeeper; 8 outside contacts; an 11-member volunteer board; 4 programs; about 1,400 donors and 6,200 gifts; about 30 grants a year; about 180 volunteers; about 2 years of history)

The demo organization is "Harbourlight Community Trust" (fictional): a small community nonprofit with a yearly budget of about 1.3M. The executive director (Grace Adeyemi, NP-01) leads; a development manager (Mateus Oliveira, NP-02) runs donors, grants and events; a program manager (Priya Nair, NP-03) runs the programs; a volunteer coordinator (Lars Henriksen, NP-04) runs shifts; three part-time program assistants (NP-05, NP-06, NP-07) and a part-time bookkeeper (Fiona Gallagher, NP-08) complete the staff; an outside accountant, a bank contact, an insurance broker, an event venue, a caterer, a printer and mail house, a foundation program officer and a corporate sponsor contact (NP-09..NP-16) are outside contacts. An 11-member volunteer board (BM-01..BM-11) has terms and three committees: finance, fundraising and governance. Four programs run all year: After-School Learning, Family Food Pantry, Senior Meals and Visits and Summer Youth Camp (summer only), plus management and fundraising costs. There are about 1,400 donors (individuals, households, businesses, foundations, faith and community groups) giving one-time and monthly through five channels, 49 pledges paid in instalments, matching gifts and in-kind gifts. About 30 grants a year move through stages with payment schedules, grant budgets and reports due. Money sits in 10 funds: General (unrestricted), a board-designated Facilities Reserve and restricted funds by grant or program, with releases from restriction as money is spent. Four fundraising events run each year: a spring gala, a fun run, a giving day and a year-end appeal. About 180 volunteers are active, with shifts and hours. Participants are kept as names, contact status, counts and dates only, with program outcomes. Staff time is split across grants, programs, management and fundraising, and a monthly cash forecast shows months of operating reserve. A founder with volunteers and no staff uses the same sheets with one program, one fund and a short donor list: each Start here tab says so.
