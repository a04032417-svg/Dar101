4. Nail/lash/beauty pro: 8 reviews, shop 7.2k sales, but item is partly printable appointment book; bookkeeping listings show no counts.
5. Therapist private practice: 5 reviews, shop 5.9k sales at $17.59 (BCBA niche); only one proven listing.
Event planner (professional): rank ~12+ of those tested; best pro listings have 0 reviews (shops 17 and 188 sales). Only consumer wedding planners sell (30+ reviews), which are excluded.
