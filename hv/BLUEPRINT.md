# HVAC and Plumbing Contractor Business suite — eight parts (all share hv_core.py: owner, office manager who also dispatches, 8 technicians, 4 outside contacts and 2 advisers, 6 vans, about 1,400 customer sites, ~2 years of history)

The demo business is "Northline Comfort & Plumbing" (fictional): an owner-run residential and light-commercial service shop. The owner (Marcus Delgado, HV-01) runs the business, the office manager (Priya Raman, HV-02) answers calls, books and dispatches. Eight technicians work from six vans: a lead HVAC tech (Tomasz Nowak, HV-03), two HVAC technicians (Kofi Mensah HV-04, Elena Rossi HV-05), two service plumbers (Jamal Whitfield HV-06, Sven Andersson HV-07), an install crew lead (Diego Morales HV-08) and two apprentices (Amira Haddad HV-09, Liam O'Connell HV-10). Outside contacts are your equipment supplier rep (HV-11), your parts counter (HV-12), an electrical subcontractor (HV-13), a sheet-metal subcontractor (HV-14), the accountant (HV-15) and the insurance agent (HV-16). About 1,400 customer sites (homes 62%, rentals managed for landlords 24%, small shops 9%, small offices 5%, with landlord and business accounts LA-01..LA-14) carry about 1,900 pieces of installed equipment (furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, boilers, water heaters, mini-splits; make, model and serial as plain fields). Work is priced from a flat-rate price book with good-better-best options (57 tasks in 15 job types): service calls with a diagnostic fee, repairs, tune-ups, replacements and installs (permits and inspections in generic words), drain, water heater and repipe work. About 430 maintenance agreements over two years (about 300 running now) bring two seasonal tune-up visits a year. Installs take a 30% deposit, some are paid through a financing offer (generic words), equipment comes from your equipment supplier and parts from your parts counter on 30-day terms, and electrical and sheet-metal work is subcontracted. Heating (December to February) and cooling (June to August) are strong, spring and autumn are slow, and cold and hot spells bring emergency calls. A one-person owner-operator with one van can use most parts: the call log, price book, estimates, job costing, van stock, agreements, invoices and cash sheets work with one technician, one van and no dispatcher; a shop with a separate dispatcher uses the dispatcher columns and the dispatch board. Every number is relative to the build day and comes from hv_core.
