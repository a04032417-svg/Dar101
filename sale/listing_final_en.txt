About Dar
Dar is an integrated cloud technology ecosystem for the real-estate sector. It brings real-estate businesses, property developers, service providers, marketers and programmers together in a single operating environment.
The platform includes management and operations systems, an AI development assistant, an in-house automation engine, business and technical portals, and API integrations.
Dar is designed to work in every country and in every language.

2) Services marked "coming soon" are ready to be activated
This is one of Dar's most important technical strengths.
Services shown as "coming soon" are not necessarily services that need to be programmed or built from scratch.
They are set up inside the platform's architecture; services that rely on external providers need those providers' connection details and API keys to be added to activate them.
Examples:
• Social-media connections.
• Messaging and chat services.
• Online payment gateways.
• E-invoicing.
• Advertising platforms.
• Voice-calling services.
• External data and information providers.
Dar's architecture lets you control services — show, activate or customise them — per interface, plan and country.
The buyer therefore gets a service architecture ready to scale, instead of having to build every integration from the beginning.
