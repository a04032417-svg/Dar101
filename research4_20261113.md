## Top 2

1. Professional wedding/event planner: the only profession with real per-listing evidence (10,830 sales, 99 in 30 days, 1,111 reviews on one listing; 26 reviews and a 3.4k-sale shop on a vendor payment tracker).

## Caveats

- Wedding evidence is weak for the "professional" angle: the 10,830-sale listing is titled as a planner for couples (budget, guest list, seating) at $9.78. It shows demand for wedding spreadsheets, not proven demand from professional planners at $29.
- Every profession-dedicated listing for cleaning, bakery, pet, grooming, landscaping shows 0 sales and 0 reviews, so there is no sales evidence for them (they may simply be new listings; item numbers 45xxxxxxxx to 46xxxxxxxx are recent).
- Strongest sales are in generic small-business client/bookkeeping trackers (hundreds of reviews, tens of thousands of shop sales) at $3 to $17, which suggests buyers pay little for spreadsheets; a $29 price is far above everything seen.
- The findniche "Store Rating 475" is likely 4.75, not sales. Etsy page metadata prices sometimes conflicted with displayed prices; displayed USD price used.
- WebSearch returned mostly unlabeled Etsy listing URLs, so coverage of ~15 professions was not achievable within the 25-call cap; only about 10 listings were opened. Shop sales are all-time and cover non-spreadsheet items too.
