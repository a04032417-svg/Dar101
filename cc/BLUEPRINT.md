# Home Daycare and Childcare Center suite — eight parts (all share cc_core.py: owner-director, assistant director who also bills, 4 lead teachers, 3 assistants, a cook, 2 part-time floaters, 4 outside contacts, 4 age rooms plus a school-age group, about 52 children enrolled, ~2 years of history)

The demo business is "Willow Lane Early Learning" (fictional): a small licensed childcare center run by an owner-director (Marisol Vega, CC-01) with an assistant director who also does the billing (Tobias Lindgren, CC-02). Four lead teachers (CC-03..CC-06, one per age room), three assistants (CC-07..CC-09), a cook (CC-10) and two part-time floaters (CC-11, CC-12) make up the staff; an accountant, a food supplier, a cleaner and a maintenance contact (CC-13..CC-16) are outside contacts. There are four age rooms (Infants, Toddlers, Twos, Preschool) plus a School-age group that runs before and after school, a summer camp and holiday camps. About 52 children are enrolled now (about 90 over two years, from about 83 families) on full-time and part-time schedules (full or half days, 2, 3 or 5 days a week). Tuition is billed weekly (about a third of families, due Friday) or monthly (issued on the 1st, due 14 days later), with a 10% sibling discount, a 25% staff discount, a registration fee, a September supply fee, a deposit of one week's tuition and a late-pickup fee after closing time. Some families are paid partly by a government childcare payment (generic words). Meals are cooked on site (breakfast, lunch, afternoon snack) with a meal-program claim (generic words). A single home daycare provider can use most parts: one room, mixed ages, the same sheets (the home-use share of the house is a Part 8 tool). Every number is relative to the build day and comes from cc_core.
