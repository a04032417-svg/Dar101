# Car Rental Business suite — eight parts (all share cr_core.py: owner-operator, a manager who also does bookings and billing, 2 detailers, 2 part-time delivery drivers, a weekend booking assistant, a part-time bookkeeper, 8 outside contacts, a fleet of 33 vehicles over two years (28 now) in 6 classes, ~2 years of history)

The demo business is "Brightwater Car & Van Rental" (fictional): a small independent car and van rental run by an owner-operator (Nadia Haddad, CR-01) with a manager who also does bookings and billing (Tomas Kowalski, CR-02). Two detailers (CR-03, CR-04), two part-time delivery drivers (CR-05, CR-06), a weekend booking assistant (CR-07) and a part-time bookkeeper (CR-08) make up the staff; an accountant, an insurance broker, a mechanic workshop, a tyre supplier, a body repair shop, a towing service, a car-wash supplier and a lender (CR-09..CR-16) are outside contacts. The fleet is 33 vehicles over two years (28 in service now, 5 sold, 13 bought during the history, a little over half financed) in 6 classes: Economy, Compact, Midsize SUV, 7-seat Van, Premium and Electric, with generic names ("Economy 01 Silver") and fake plates. There is one main lot plus airport, hotel-district, hotel-strip and home delivery zones. Daily, weekly and monthly rentals are booked direct (website, phone, corporate accounts, a hotel partner) and through two car-sharing marketplaces (generic words: Marketplace A and Marketplace B). Rates follow a seasonal card (off-peak, shoulder, peak, holiday) with a weekend uplift; direct bookings carry a deposit held on a card; add-ons are a child seat, an extra driver, a navigation unit, two cover options (generic words), a young-driver fee and a roof box; fees are extra mileage, late return, cleaning, fuel or charge refill and toll and ticket admin. Damage claims are kept as status and amounts only. Maintenance follows a service interval in miles and months; registration, insurance and inspection renewals are tracked per vehicle. A one-car host starting out can use most of every part (one vehicle, one channel, the same sheets). Every number is relative to the build day and comes from cr_core.
