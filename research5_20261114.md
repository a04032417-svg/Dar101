## Top 15 ranked (evidence is thin; ranking = repeated seller activity + weekly jobs + low overlap)

1. Home bakery / cake business - most repeated niche in best-volume shop (TrackerPro has 3 bakery listings); weekly jobs: orders, recipe costing, pricing, deposits.
2. Real estate investor (rental, BRRRR, flip) - only niche priced $33+ with a 1.5k-sale shop; but keep distinct from landlord (deal analysis, rehab budget, portfolio).
3. Candle / soap / handmade maker - repeated in multiple shops; batch costing, inventory, wholesale, craft-fair weeks.
4. Lash / nail / esthetician / beauty studio - salon bookkeeping in top shop; appointments, retail, tips, rebooking; avoid copy of hair salon by using service-menu/consumables focus.
5. Freelancer / consultant - highest repeat across shops; proposals, time, invoices, taxes (check overlap with accounting).
6. Content creator / influencer (brand deals) - several dedicated products on Etsy/Gumroad/Stan; deals, deliverables, rates, income.
7. Pet grooming / pet sitting / dog walking - one shop built a 4-product pet line; weekly bookings, vaccines, routes.
8. Daycare / childcare provider - ledger families exist; weekly attendance, tuition, parent billing.
9. Tax preparer - in top shop bundle; client intake, seasons, fees (more seasonal).
10. Notary / loan signing agent - dedicated listing families; journal, mileage, fees per signing.
11. Lawn care / landscaping - estimates, routes, seasonal contracts; clear weekly jobs.
12. Therapist / counsellor / clinical private practice - clinical-hours trackers repeated by seller; sessions, notes-free billing, supervision hours.
13. Florist - floral business template in niche shop; orders, events, flower costing, waste.
14. Virtual assistant - no direct evidence; clients, retainers, hours; fits freelancer line.
15. Tutoring / music / yoga studio - no direct evidence; recurring lessons, packages, attendance (pick one: yoga/pilates studio).

## Caveats
- NO listing-level sales figure for any candidate profession was found. Only findniche (wedding, budget) and Etsy shop totals gave numbers; shop totals mix many products.
- Etsy market pages and search were not accessible; fresh listings (new shops with 0-6 sales) prove supply, not demand.
- TrackerPro prices at $3.48 sale; its 19.7k sales are volume at near-zero price and cannot be taken as $29 demand.
- Seller repetition (same niche listed several times) is used as a proxy for sales; it is a weak signal.
- Real estate investor shops found were tiny (17 sales); the one 1.5k-sale shop is not mainly spreadsheets for this profession alone.
- Recommend a follow-up with findniche or erank (login) to verify items 1-8 before building.
