# Pet grooming business suite — eight parts (all share pg_core.py: owner-groomer, 3 salon groomers, 1 mobile van groomer, 2 bathers, 1 front-desk coordinator, 8 outside contacts, 4 grooming tables plus the van, about 500 grooms a month, ~2 years of history)

The demo business is "Willowbrook Pet Grooming" (fictional): a grooming salon run by an owner-groomer (Rosa Delgado, PG-01) with three salon groomers (Tomas Eriksen on 50% commission, Mei Tanaka hourly plus a small bonus, Chidi Adeyemi part-time on a fixed 60% commission), two bathers (Ana Popescu, Kwame Mensah), a front-desk coordinator (Aisha Rahman) and one mobile grooming van with its own groomer (Lucia Ferreira, 50% commission, route days Tuesday to Friday). Eight outside contacts: accountant, grooming-supply wholesaler, clipper and dryer repair service, van mechanic, landlord, laundry service, insurance broker, print and sign shop. About 830 clients (about 650 active in the last 90 days) with about 960 pets, mostly dogs and some cats; recurring clients rebook every 4-8 weeks, walk-ins come for nail trims. Services are priced by size band (Small, Medium, Large, Giant) and coat type, with add-ons and matting and handling surcharges; new clients pay a deposit; late cancels and no-shows are charged a fee; groomers are paid by commission share or by the hour; tips go to the groomer; a retail shelf and back-bar product use per groom; clippers, blades, dryers, tubs and the van (fuel, water, generator, route days) have service dates. **A solo groomer, salon or mobile, can use most parts: hide the staff, bather and station columns, keep one groomer row, and every page still works (the help text of each part says which pages a solo groomer keeps).** Each part is a standalone workbook that carries its own copies of the reference tables it needs. Charts group by month or by a list column whose values the demo data uses fully (groomer, service, size, coat, channel, status, area, station, reason, source, kind, category); never by a calc column, an id, a client or pet name, a quarter or a year list, and never with hundreds of rows as an axis. A table with fewer than 12 rows may have only 3 columns: design for that (Services, Coats, Add-ons, Surcharges, Areas, Stations). Health and records: the workbook holds dates and status only — a vaccination record date and its status (Current, Due soon, Expired), a consent signed date, an incident log as the owner observed it — never a diagnosis, a health claim or advice; wherever a question about a pet's health arises the text says "ask your vet". No brand, software, platform, bank, equipment-maker, product or company names and no law, agency, licence or standard names anywhere.
