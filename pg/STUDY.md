# STUDY — small pet grooming business (salon with 2-6 staff, mobile van, or solo groomer): market and workflow

Method: 25 web calls (searches and fetches) on 2026-11-15, building on research6_20261115.md (pet lines and Research 10), not repeating it. Evidence is thin in places and says so. Many search hits were vendor blogs, which are marketing-grade. Forum threads (Reddit, groomer groups) were NOT verified: searches returned vendor and trade-magazine pages, not readable posts. Brand names appear here only as research sources; none may reach the product.

## 1. Real jobs and pain points

**Daily jobs (owner, front desk, groomers):** open the day sheet (who grooms which dog, start times, expected finish); check each pet profile for temperament and handling notes and any matting or behaviour flag; confirm arrivals and vaccination-record dates (date and status only); log drop-off condition and consent for the day; run the groom and watch the clock against the booked time; take payment and tips; rebook at checkout (offer two slots); send pick-up message; log incidents; note retail sold and product used; close the till and daily take (cash, card, tips, deposits). Mobile: confirm route and drive times, fill water, check fuel and generator, note gate or parking details, groom one dog at a time, log mileage.
**Weekly jobs:** check next week's calendar for holes and fill from the waiting or cancellation list; chase unconfirmed bookings and no-shows; rebooking gap check (clients with no next visit, clients past their usual 4-8 week interval); groomer hours and commission pre-check; retail reorder; equipment check (blades to sharpen, dryer filters, tub drains, clipper oil); laundry and supplies; review requests and replies; weekly cash check; mobile: plan route days by area.
**Monthly jobs:** pay run (commission share, hourly, tips handed on); revenue by groomer, by service and by hour; profit and loss; supplier invoices; retail stock count and margin; lapsed-client list (no visit in 10-12 weeks) and win-back messages; deposits held versus forfeited; blade and equipment replacement set-aside; marketing spend and new-client source check; tax set-aside transfer; owner pay.
**Yearly jobs:** price list rise and notice; holiday and seasonal peak plan (books fill weeks before major holidays, summer shed and matting season); insurance and licence renewals (say "your local rules"); equipment replacement budget; van service and replacement plan (mobile); review of commission or hourly pay terms; training plan for bathers; year-end pack for the accountant; client list clean-up; budget.

**Pain points reported (sources):**
- Owners lose money on 50% commission once overhead is counted: one owner says a new salon lost money under 50% commission; commission-based pay "no longer works" for some; an expert says an employee costs 12-15% more than the wage (payroll taxes and extras); alternatives discussed: hourly with a $15 starting wage, hourly plus production bonus, guaranteed wage plus smaller commission, profit sharing. https://digital.groomertogroomer.com/issue/september-2025/commission-based-pay-for-groomers-why-it-no-longer-works/
- Groomers fixate on "50%": recruited groomers insisted on 50%; "how can owners quote a commission without a dollar amount?" (same source).
- No-shows and late cancels cost the slot and the chance to fill it from a waiting list; owners use strikes (client barred from booking for 4 weeks after a no-show or late cancel; one warning for trusted clients). https://www.moego.pet/blog/nomore-noshows
- Clients cancel same day despite a 24-hour rule and the software does not charge automatically (reviewer: "Clients are able to cancel same day despite me having 24 hour notice."). https://capterra.com/p/165732/MoeGo/reviews/
- "We'll call you" at checkout: clients leave without rebooking; the author calls it "a polite goodbye" and a six-week interval stretches to ten. https://thedailygroomer.beehiiv.com/p/stop-asking-clients-to-rebook-do-this-instead
- Mobile: travel time cuts income; repairs and downtime lose clients; weather and slow seasons; extra insurance; daily water, power, grey-water and bathroom logistics; mobile groomers "often undervalue their services by pricing against salons". https://digital.groomertogroomer.com/issue/july-2026/making-the-move-to-mobile-is-it-right-for-you/
- Owners often do not know their numbers: "many salon closures" attributed to poor money management and unrealistic expectations; rising insurance, utility and rent squeeze owners (Groomer to Groomer, Sept 2025, same commission article).
- Matting and extra labour are not priced consistently: advice is to add dematting time charges as a pricing modifier. https://www.moego.pet/blog/how-much-is-dog-grooming ; UK sources quote flat dematting fees of 10-30 pounds or about 1 pound per minute (weak, unnamed source). https://tailly-landing.pages.dev/blog/pricing-and-profitability/what-should-dog-groomers-charge-uk-2025
- Software gaps in reviewers' words: "reports and analytics could be more detailed", "we wish we had access to more data", "pricing setup for breeds takes lots of clicking" (Capterra, MoeGo); "reports should be easy to customize, they are not", "We cannot see or update vaccines on app ( this is very important)" (Capterra, DaySmart Pet).
- NOT verified: Reddit and Facebook groomer-group posts (not retrievable).

## 2. Leading grooming / pet-business software (research only)
Monthly prices seen:
- MoeGo (Capterra pricing page): Mobile Basic $49; Salon Basic $79; Mobile Growth $99; Salon Growth $149; boarding plans custom. Flat per plan, no per-van price listed, no free tier, no free trial. https://www.capterra.com/p/165732/MoeGo/pricing/
- Pawfinity (Capterra pricing): $50 (daycare/boarding), $50 (grooming and training, includes routes), $90 (all, 250 free texts). Flat. https://www.capterra.com/p/142461/Pawfinity/pricing/
- DaySmart Pet: price not shown on Capterra; a reviewer says value "changed vs what we were quoted", another switched after a previous provider raised prices 70%.
- Gingr, Groomsoft, Groomore, 123Pet: prices NOT verified (pages listed, not fetched).
Features offered: online booking, auto reminders and notifications, two-way texts, client and pet profiles, stored cards, point of sale, inventory, time clock and payroll, routes and mapping (mobile), review booster, document storage, add-on services, waitlist.
User complaints (Capterra; MoeGo 4.7 of 5 from 232 reviews; DaySmart Pet 4.3 from 226 reviews; Pawfinity 4.6 from 107 reviews, complaints not read):
- Cost: "I pay 250 a month and have to use work arounds?" (1 star); extra text messages cost; review booster and salary calculations only on the higher plan; a Canadian user says dollar billing plus card fees made real cost about 10% higher; an early-cancellation fee when leaving.
- Support: slow, chat or bot only; bugs; outages.
- Scheduling: same-day cancels not charged automatically; moving appointments is hard; waitlist "does not work for our set up"; cannot set two phone numbers for reminders.
- Data and reports: more data wanted, reports hard to customize, client records must be merged by hand, pets cannot easily move to a new owner.
- Money: no sole-trader accounting link; card payment times out if you forget to press complete.
- Switching: one reviewer went back to the old product, tasks "5x more complicated".
**What a spreadsheet must do better:** one flat 29 dollars, no per-groomer or per-van fee, no text-message add-on charges; own your client list and history; automatic late-cancel and deposit charge sheet (amount owed per no-show, ready to bill); one-screen commission or hourly pay check per groomer with all-in cost; profit per service, per breed, per hour and per groomer (software shows sales, not margin); matting and behaviour surcharge calculator in minutes; rebooking and lapsed-client list that ranks who to call first; works for a solo groomer and a van; mobile day profit with fuel, water and generator; simple import and export so owners can leave at any time.

## Gaps and improvements for the blueprint
(Most valuable first. Keep every item generic: no brand, law, licence or product names.)

1. **True price per groom.** Calculator from size, coat type, coat condition and add-ons to a price: base by breed class, minutes needed, surcharges for matting (per 10 minutes) and behaviour handling, rounded to the owner's step; show the price against the owner's minimum price per hour. Verdict cell: "price ok", "raise", "refuse or reprice".
2. **Revenue per groomer hour.** For every groom: price / minutes actually worked; ranked table of services and breeds from best to worst per hour; flags groomed jobs below the target hour value.
3. **Commission or hourly: which is cheaper?** Inputs: groomer sales, hours, split share, hourly wage, bonus, employer on-cost (default 12-15%, editable). Output: all-in cost per groomer and per groom under each pay type, with margin left and a verdict. Owners complain 50% loses money once overhead is counted.
4. **Groomer pay sheet.** Daily and monthly: services done, commission on service price (before or after discount, setting), tips passed on, hours, guaranteed minimum top-up, deductions; one-page statement per groomer.
5. **Groomer comparison by hour and by dog.** Dogs per day, minutes per groom vs booked time, revenue per hour, rebooking rate, complaints or redo count per groomer, ranked.
6. **No-show and late-cancel cost.** Count and money lost per month, by weekday, by new vs repeat client; "what a deposit would have saved" scenario; forecast of yearly loss at current rate.
7. **Deposit policy tester.** Inputs: deposit amount or percent, no-show rate now, expected rate after; output: net money recovered per month and a verdict (keep, change, add card on file). Rate defaults are the owner's own, not the weak 10-15% to 2-5% claim.
8. **Late-cancel invoice list.** Auto list of bookings cancelled inside the window with the fee to bill, message text, and strike count; bar client after N strikes (setting).
9. **Deposits ledger.** Held, applied, forfeited, refunded by booking; balance held at month end as a liability line.
10. **Rebooking gap list.** Every client's last visit, usual interval, next due date and "days overdue"; ranked call list with the value of each client.
11. **Rebook-at-checkout rate** by groomer and by front-desk person with the 70% line (setting) and a gauge; track "we'll call you" outcomes.
12. **Lapsed clients and win-back.** Clients with no visit in 1.5 times their interval, value lost, a ranked win-back list, result column (returned or not), and the money won back.
13. **Client lifetime value.** Visits per year x average ticket x years; shows what one lost client costs and what a referral reward may be.
14. **Retention by cohort.** New clients per month and share still booked at 3, 6 and 12 months; compare with own history, not a published number.
15. **Pet profile card.** Breed, coat, size, usual interval, handling notes and temperament (owner-reported observations), no-show history, last price; one printable card per pet.
16. **Vaccination record dates.** Date and status only (current, due in 30 days, expired); alert list for tomorrow's bookings; "ask your vet" note; no health words.
17. **Consent and incident log.** Consent signed on (date), special handling agreed, incident log (date, groomer, what happened as observed, what was told to the owner, follow-up); counts by groomer.
18. **Day sheet and capacity.** Groomers x hours x minutes per groom: slots left today and this week, overbooking check, and an "add a dog?" tool that answers yes or no from the time left.
19. **Slot filler for cancellations.** Ranked waiting list (overdue clients, service length that fits the gap, value); one-click message text list.
20. **Booking calendar grid.** Week view per groomer or per van with colour by service length and a clash check; solo groomer view hides the groomer column.
21. **Daily take sheet.** Cash, card, deposits applied, tips, retail; variance against bookings completed; end-of-day close with a signature line.
22. **Tips ledger.** Tips by groomer, pooled or individual (setting), paid out with the pay run.
23. **Retail stock and margin.** Cost, price, margin, stock, reorder point; ranking by margin and by speed of sale; dead-stock list; retail share of revenue as an own-history KPI (no external number).
24. **Back-bar product use.** Product cost per groom by size (shampoo, conditioner, blades wear, laundry, water); sets a cost per service line and feeds the price calculator.
25. **Equipment and blade schedule.** Each clipper, blade set, dryer, tub: purchase date, uses, next sharpening or service, replacement cost; monthly set-aside amount; alert list for the week.
26. **Equipment replace-or-repair.** Inputs repair cost, age, downtime days lost x revenue per day; verdict repair or replace.
27. **Service profit report.** Revenue, direct labour, product cost and margin by service (full groom, hand-strip, de-shed, bath only, cat, add-ons), ranked; losing service finder with the price that breaks even.
28. **Breed profit report.** Same by breed class: time vs price, so owners see which dogs cost more time than they earn.
29. **Add-on attach rate.** Share of grooms with at least one add-on, by groomer, with revenue per add-on and a target in settings (default 75%, labelled opinion).
30. **Matting and behaviour surcharge log.** Count and money by groomer; share of surcharges collected vs given away; verdict "you gave away N dollars in free dematting".
31. **Seasonal peak planner.** From last year's monthly bookings: expected weeks of full books, holiday cut-off dates, whether to add hours, a temporary bather, full prepay or deposit rule for peak days; forecast of extra revenue vs extra labour.
32. **Price rise tool.** Raise every price by x% or by size; show clients lost break-even (how many clients can leave before the rise loses money), and the date for notice.
33. **Mobile day costing.** Per route day: grooms, drive minutes and miles, fuel, water, generator hours, van wear per mile, insurance share; profit per day and per groom; verdict on the travel fee and the minimum number of grooms per day.
34. **Mobile route-day planner.** Area per weekday, grooms booked, drive time between, empty-slot gap list; compares "area A on Tuesday" with "area B on Thursday" by profit per hour including driving.
35. **Van running cost.** Fixed (loan, insurance, licence, service) and variable (fuel, water, power) per month and per working day; fuel surcharge trigger table.
36. **Cash flow forecast and cash floor.** 12 weeks ahead from bookings, deposits, rent, pay runs, supplier bills; low-cash warning; months of cover.
37. **Owner pay, profit and tax set-aside.** Profit after real labour (owner's own groom hours charged at a wage), set-aside percent in settings ("confirm with your adviser"), owner pay affordable this month and a verdict.
38. **Break-even and capacity scenario.** Stations (or vans) x grooms per day x days x average ticket vs fixed costs; "what if one more groomer", "what if no-shows fall by 5 points", "what if price +8%" side by side.
39. **Hiring and training a bather.** Business case: wage, training hours, grooms freed for the groomer, added revenue per week, months to pay back; trainee skill checklist (task, date shown, date signed off); probation tracker.
40. **Reviews and referrals.** Review requests sent, received, average rating, referral source per new client, and value of each source; ranked sources; "which source brings paying regulars" as first line.

Plus cross-cutting: each workbook has a Start here tab, Settings with editable targets, sample plus blank copy, "replace demo data" steps, no macros, Excel and Google Sheets use, solo-groomer view (hide staff columns), and "compare with my own history" rows on monthly reports.

**Suggested 8 part titles (no overlap):**
1. Bookings, Calendar and Day Sheet (slots, capacity, waiting list, walk-ins, route days view)
2. Pricing and Service Menu (price calculator, surcharges, price rise, service and breed profit)
3. Clients, Pets and Rebooking (profiles, handling notes, vaccination dates, rebooking, lapsed clients, retention)
4. No-shows, Deposits and Records (cancel rules, deposit ledger, consent and incident log)
5. Groomers, Pay and Training (commission or hourly, tips, comparison by groomer, bather hiring and training)
6. Daily Take, Retail and Back-bar Stock (till, tips ledger, shelf stock, product use per groom, reorder)
7. Equipment, Van and Mobile Costing (blade and equipment schedule, repair or replace, van running cost, route-day profit)
8. Money, Seasons and Owner Pay (profit and loss, cash flow, tax set-aside, owner pay, peak-season planner, scenarios, reviews and referral sources)

## Product wording rules
- No brand, software, platform, bank or company names anywhere in the product (including competitors, payment providers, marketplaces).
- No law, regulation, agency, licence, programme or standard names: say "your local rules", "your licence or insurance renewal", "sales tax or VAT/GST as your local rules call it", "confirm with your adviser".
- No veterinary or medical claims and no diagnosis words. Health notes are "owner-reported observations" only; vaccination records are dates and status only (current, due soon, expired); add "ask your vet" where a question arises. Service names like "anal gland" or "flea bath" are not used in the product unless as a neutral add-on label chosen by the owner; prefer "sanitary", "extra treatment".
- No shampoo, product or equipment brand names; use "shampoo", "blades", "high-velocity dryer".
- Gender-neutral wording: "the client", "the groomer", "they"; no "he/she", no "owner's wife".
- Accept regional words as labels (groomer, parlour, pet salon, mobile groomer, takings, sole trader) without naming countries' agencies or rules.
- Competitor and benchmark figures from this study are for the blueprint only; in the product, targets are editable "your number" settings with plain-language labels; weak numbers (no-show rates, deposit effects, retail share) are never presented as industry facts.
- No invented reviews, sales claims or testimonials in the shop listing or the workbooks.
