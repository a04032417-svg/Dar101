# Wedding Planning suite for couples — eight parts (all share wp_core.py: one wedding, 150 guests in 82 households, 16 vendors, 140 tasks, 8 events, a wedding party of 10 with 4 family helpers, 13 tables; about 9 months of history and about 200 days still to go)

The demo wedding is "Amara and Theo" (partner 1 and partner 2, fictional): an engaged couple planning their own wedding, not professional planners. They got engaged about 9 months ago; the wedding day (a Saturday) is about 200 days after today, so the demo always sits mid-planning. One venue holds the ceremony and the reception, with a seated capacity of 130. 150 guests are invited in 82 households (both sides plus shared friends, A list and B list: the A list had digital invitations and save-the-dates 40 days ago because of travel and a hotel room block, the B list second wave went out 12 days ago), about 118 are expected, and the reply-by date is in 21 days. The total budget is about 34,000 in one generic currency, paid by the couple (agreed share 60%), partner 1's family (25%) and partner 2's family (15%). Events: engagement party (done), shower (done 24 days ago), two weekends away with friends, rehearsal dinner, ceremony, reception, farewell brunch. About 16 vendors in generic words (venue, caterer, photographer, videographer, florist, band or DJ, officiant, cake baker, hair and makeup, attire shop, tailor, stationery printer, rentals, transport, day-of coordinator, hotel room block) with 40 quotes, 13 booked contracts, 31 scheduled payments, deposits, final balances and tips. A master checklist of 140 tasks by month-out with owners and status; a wedding party of 10 with roles, tasks and attire status plus 4 family helpers; seating at 13 tables; a minute-by-minute run of show; 14 DIY projects with supplies; shower and engagement gifts, cash gifts and thank-you notes; a honeymoon with two options; name change and paperwork in generic words. A couple with a small wedding (30 guests, no wedding party) uses most of the same sheets: each Start here tab says so, and each help text names what to delete or leave empty (one table, one event, no party, no seating plan).

**5. Money model (every part that shows money).** One generic currency, plain numbers, no symbols or codes. Four numbers per budget line: planned (your budget), committed (signed contracts), paid (money out) and still due = committed - paid; variance = committed - planned (booked categories) with the verdict On track (variance at or below 0), Watch (up to 10% over) or Over (more than 10% over), thresholds editable. Funded = Received + Promised funding; Not confirmed cash gifts are never counted as funding until received. Per-guest and fixed items: each category carries a cost type (Per guest or Fixed) in the split bands; per-guest items scale with headcount.
